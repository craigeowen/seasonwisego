How we rank resorts

Last updated 22 November 2026

Piste Picker ranks resorts on how well they fit the answers you give: how you ride, where you're flying from, when you're going and for how long. After your riding, snow comes first.

Snow is king. In Iglu Ski's 2026 customer survey, snow reliability was the top reason UK skiers chose a resort (78%), well ahead of a quick transfer (54%). So snow carries nearly twice the weight of transfer time on a long weekend, and three times as much on a week away.

Step 1: the filters

First, we remove resorts that don't fit the basics you set:

  • Your transfer limit. Resorts further than the longest transfer you'll accept drop out.
  • Direct flights only. If ticked, a resort stays only if there's a confirmed direct route from your airport in season, or the airport is a very busy one (such as Geneva or Zurich) with flights from your area. We check easyJet, Ryanair and Jet2 flying days where we can.
  • Your budget, and the countries or resorts you've chosen to search.

Anything you picked that doesn't make the list appears under Not on your list, with the reason and a one-tap fix.

Step 2: the score

Each remaining resort gets points for each part of the match. The total, out of the most it could score, is its % match.

PartPointsWhat it measures
Riding fit50How the resort's powder, groomers, parks, steeps and trees match your Skip, Some, Keen and Must choices. Beginner and snowboarder settings count too.
Snow reliability25The share of days in your month with at least 50 cm of natural snow around the resort over the last 20 winters (2006/07 to 2025/26), topped up for resorts with good snowmaking.
Après10How close the resort's après is to the level you asked for.
Budget8The resort's price level against your budget. With "Any budget", every resort scores in full.
Transfer time15 to 8Shorter is better. It counts most on short trips: 15 points for 3 nights or fewer, 12 for 4 nights, 10 for 5 to 6 nights, 8 for a week or more.

Some settings add points: up to 8 for a country you prefer, 12 for a resort you already have in mind, and up to 8 for things to do off the slopes if not everyone in your group skis or rides.

Some take points off: up to 8 when few flights serve the resort from your airport, and up to 14 when your dates clash with school holidays and you've asked to avoid them.

Why snow beats transfer

  • It's what skiers say matters most (see the survey above).
  • You already control transfer time with your transfer limit. Snow has no filter, so the score is where it counts.
  • A short transfer is little use without snow. On a long weekend, though, every hour on a coach is an hour off the hill, which is why transfer counts for more on short trips.

How the % is shown

Resorts are ranked on their exact score. When two neighbours would round to the same %, we nudge the shown numbers apart, by no more than 2 points, so the list reads in order. If two resorts are exactly level, the shorter transfer goes first. Tap Why X%? on any resort to see every point and the exact score.

Commission never affects the rankings. Whether a resort has partner links, or what a partner pays, plays no part in the order. See how we make money.

Where the data comes from

  • Snow: 20 winters of historical snow depth around each resort, from Open-Meteo, topped up for snowmaking. It describes the wider area rather than individual pistes. Sierra Nevada and Bansko use an estimate instead.
  • Flights: airline timetables and route pages, checked monthly.
  • Resort ratings (terrain, après, price level) and transfer times: our own research, compiled for this beta.

The ratings and weights are our best judgement, not a law of nature. If a resort looks ranked too high or too low, please tell us: that's how we improve it.
