    About Piste Picker

    Piste Picker is a free ski trip planner for skiers and snowboarders flying from the UK and Ireland. Tell it your airport, dates, group and how you like to ride, and it ranks 90 European resorts across the Alps, Pyrenees, Scandinavia and beyond, with the reasons for each. It checks which direct flights run on your dates (with times where we have them), how long the transfer takes, how snow-sure each resort has been in your month over 20 winters, and whether your dates clash with UK school holidays.
