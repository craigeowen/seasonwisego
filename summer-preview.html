What you're after

Ryanair, easyJet and Jet2 don't carry pet dogs, and most UK holidays with a dog are by car, so we've switched you to Drive. You can switch back to Fly if you've found an airline that takes your dog.

Taking your dog from the UK
  • Before you go: a new Animal Health Certificate from your vet for every trip, issued no more than 10 days before you travel. Since 22 April 2026, GB residents can't use an EU pet passport to enter the EU.
  • Also needed: a microchip, and a rabies jab at least 21 days before you go.
  • Coming home: a vet must treat your dog for tapeworm 1 to 5 days before you arrive back in Great Britain (not needed coming straight from Norway).
  • Crossing: LeShuttle through the Channel Tunnel and the Dover ferries take dogs in your car.

Rules change, so check GOV.UK: taking your pet abroad before you book.
